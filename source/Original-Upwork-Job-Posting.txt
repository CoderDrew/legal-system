Legal Operations AI MVP / Legacy Legal System AI Overhaul - Senior AI Product Engineer

Worldwide

Summary
We are an established law firm looking for a senior AI product engineer / full-stack developer to design and build a production-ready MVP that uses AI to streamline internal legal and operational workflows.

This is not a basic chatbot project.

We want to build an internal AI operations platform that can work with firm data, documents, emails and existing business systems to reduce repetitive administrative work, improve knowledge retrieval and help our team work more efficiently.

We are looking for someone who can own the product end to end:

business problem
→ workflow design
→ architecture
→ AI/model selection
→ full-stack development
→ integrations
→ testing
→ deployment
→ iteration with real users

Founder, technical founder and product-builder backgrounds are particularly welcome.

AI safety and reliability

Because this system will be used inside a law firm, reliability is more important than producing an answer every time.

We are especially interested in developers who understand the failure modes of LLM systems.

The system must be able to distinguish between:

known information
→ inferred information
→ uncertain information
→ missing information

An AI assistant should be allowed to say that it does not know.

It should not turn uncertainty into confident legal or operational statements.

Architecture philosophy

We expect the platform to separate AI reasoning from business-critical rules.

A typical workflow may look like:

user request
→ authenticated user/matter context
→ approved firm data
→ retrieval
→ deterministic rules
→ AI/model layer
→ structured output
→ validation
→ human approval where required
→ action
→ audit log

LLMs should interpret, classify, summarise and assist.

They should not become the uncontrolled source of truth for sensitive business decisions.
