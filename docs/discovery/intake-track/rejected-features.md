# Intake: Rejected and Out-of-Scope Features

**Status: DISCOVERY TRACK. NOT APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION.** See [`README.md`](./README.md).

This file lists every intake feature the team proposed and then ruled out, with who ruled it out and why. Sources: [proposal §3 and §4.7](./intake-v1-proposal-parked.md), plus the team analyses and Dial-Tone's safety and testability verdicts (condensed in [`team-analysis-summary.md`](./team-analysis-summary.md)).

**How to read it**
- **Proposed by / Ruled out by** use team role names: Recondo (operations), Mainframe (systems), Dial-Tone (failure modes), Filecard (workflow record and citation audit), Duke (synthesis).
- **DT verdict** is Dial-Tone's cross-challenge verdict. *Safety FAIL* means a wrong output could cause a silent conflict false negative, or a dropped lead with nothing human to catch it. *Testability PASS* means it can be checked against known ground truth on synthetic data alone.
- Every reason is a HYPOTHESIS or CONSTRAINT unless it is marked FACT. Every Clio capability is **UNVERIFIED** for this firm.
- **CONSTRAINT (Oct 2, 2026):** after option (c), even the features the proposal kept *in* scope (Part A and the Intake Packet) are parked and not approved. They are listed at the end for completeness.
